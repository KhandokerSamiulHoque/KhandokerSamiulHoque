### Hi, I'm Samiul

I'm a power electronics engineer in Fremont, California, working on solar inverter and PV equipment testing. On my own time I build machine learning tools for problems I see in that work: spotting cell defects in electroluminescence images, checking whether inverter models are honest about their accuracy, and keeping grid-edge devices safe from network attacks.

I have an MSEE from San Francisco Bay University (2024) and a BSc in EEE from Brac University (2021). I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

#### Research code

| Repository | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [pv-el-defect-screening](https://github.com/KhandokerSamiulHoque/pv-el-defect-screening) | Interpretable defect screening on 2,624 ELPV solar-cell images. F1 0.670 on any defect, ROC-AUC 0.816 on high-confidence defects | Published, IJSRA 20(2), 2026 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.30574/ijsra.2026.20.2.1601)) |
| [inverter-efficiency-ml](https://github.com/KhandokerSamiulHoque/inverter-efficiency-ml) | Predicts inverter efficiency from the CEC database and audits target leakage. F1 falls from 0.9994 to 0.9736 once the leak is removed | Manuscript in preparation |
| [federated-energy-ids](https://github.com/KhandokerSamiulHoque/federated-energy-ids) | Federated intrusion detection across 10 simulated sites, plus a poisoning study where coordinate median holds 0.757 accuracy against 0.251 for FedAvg | Manuscript in preparation |

Every repo states its limits and has been rerun from scratch to match the reported numbers.
